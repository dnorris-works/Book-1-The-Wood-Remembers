Description ★ Required 210 words
When quantum archaeologist Dr. Sarah Davidson discovers fragments of the original cross in a hidden cave beneath Jerusalem, she believes she's found the ultimate test case for her revolutionary MEMORIA protocol. Using cutting-edge quantum memory reading technology, Sarah can extract molecular-level imprints from ancient organic material—turning wood and bone into witnesses to history. But these fragments hold more than data. For two thousand years, an olive tree has carried testimony of the Crucifixion, waiting to be found. As Sarah's instruments probe deeper into the quantum grain of the wood, she encounters something that cannot be measured: the consciousness of a tree that stood witness to Christ's final moments. But Sarah isn't the only one interested in her discovery. When her data is stolen by a colleague who believes faith has no place in science, she must choose between protecting her career and defending what the fragments truly hold. With a Vatican commission breathing down her neck and her life's work hanging in the balance, Sarah faces an impossible decision: treat the cross as archaeological evidence, or receive it as living testimony. Experience the boundary where quantum science meets ancient faith, where measurement gives way to mystery, and where one woman must learn the difference between extracting data and receiving truth.
