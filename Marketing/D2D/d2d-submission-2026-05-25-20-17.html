Book Description ★ Required 176 words
This description is distributed to all D2D platforms simultaneously — Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, Scribd, and more. Plain text only. Paragraph breaks are preserved.
A Harvard archaeologist discovers ancient fragments that challenge everything she thought she knew about faith and science. Dr. Sarah Davidson has perfected quantum memory reading—a revolutionary technology that extracts testimony from ancient organic matter at the molecular level. When fragments of the original cross surface in a Vatican archaeological dig, Sarah's MEMORIA protocol promises to unlock the greatest archaeological discovery in history. But as her quantum archaeology reveals the consciousness of an olive tree witness to the Crucifixion, Sarah faces an impossible choice: publish data that will make her career, or receive testimony that will remake her life. From the limestone hills of ancient Judea to the laboratories of Harvard, The Quantum Cross weaves cutting-edge quantum science with timeless questions of faith. As Sarah reads deeper into the tree's two-thousand-year vigil, she discovers that some truths cannot be extracted—only received. But a principled researcher threatens to steal her work, and the Vatican commission demands proof that could destroy the very testimony she's uncovering. Will Sarah's instruments measure what happened at Calvary, or will Calvary measure her?
