## What the Columns Mean

- **Avg Pages:** Average page count of books ranking for this keyword. Helps you know what length readers in this niche expect.
- **Number of Competitors:** How many books are competing for this keyword on Amazon. Lower = easier to rank and be found.
- **Avg Price:** What competing books typically sell for. Sets realistic pricing expectations.
- **Avg Monthly Earnings:** What the top-ranking books earn per month from this keyword. Higher = more actual buyers (not just browsers).
- **Est. Amazon Searches/Month:** How many people type this phrase into Amazon each month. Higher = more potential readers seeing your book.
- **Competitive Score:** A 1–100 difficulty score. Lower = easier to rank. Higher = more established competition to fight through.

---

## The Sweet Spot Formula

The best keywords for a new author combine:
- **Decent search volume** — 300+ searches/month so real people find you
- **Lower competitive score** — under 45 ideally (you can rank without a huge backlist)
- **Real monthly earnings** — proves these are buyers, not people just browsing for free content
