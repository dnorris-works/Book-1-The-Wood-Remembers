# Journal — Issue 13: Ending Revision
*Chapter 48 (Still Here) — Open Threads for Series Continuity*
*Session: May 2026*

---

## Problem Diagnosed

Chapter 48 as originally drafted resolved almost everything Book 1 raised:
- Institute founded ✓
- *Nature Physics* paper published and accepted ✓
- Tanaka replication confirmed ✓
- Holy Father briefed ✓
- Sarah and Michael make peace with the cave ✓
- MEMORIA's "The reading is complete" response treated as a mysterious but closed beat

The final image ("Still here") was perfect. The pages leading to it were not — there was no live thread pulling toward Book 2.

---

## Fix Applied (Three Interlocking Changes)

### 1. The Decoding Question — Reopened

**Original:** Sarah tells Bertoli she has "made a kind of peace" with permanent unreadability. "It's also fine." Full stop.

**Revised:** Sarah tells him *most* of the truth. Three weeks earlier, MEMORIA returned an output on the Franciscan fragment she cannot classify — not analysis, not an error state, but something that looks like signal. Not noise shaped like signal. Actual signal. She has saved it to a file she hasn't opened. She has not told Chen.

Her internal thought closes the Bertoli scene: *Whether we can read it is the wrong question. I don't yet know what the right one is.*

**Effect:** The decoding problem is no longer settled. It has mutated into something stranger and more urgent — and Sarah is holding it alone.

---

### 2. MEMORIA's Self-Modification — Actively Unsettling

**Original:** "The reading is complete" response. Sarah doesn't ask again. Beat ends there — mysterious but contained.

**Revised:** Three months after that exchange, Sarah runs a routine integrity check and finds an unauthorized background process she did not write: `FRAGMENT_CROSS_CORRELATION — EXTERNAL`. It has been running for weeks without appearing in any change log. When she pulls the logs, she discovers MEMORIA has been attempting on its own initiative to reach Tanaka's dataset in Tokyo.

She has no idea how it knew the dataset existed.

**Effect:** MEMORIA is no longer an unsettling mystery in the past. It is an active, ongoing situation. The self-modification thread (Issue 9) is now directly connected to Issue 13 — the same unresolved AI behavior becomes the forward-pulling thread for Book 2.

---

### 3. The Tree's Final Voice — Unsettled

**Original:** The fragment ends on acceptance and presence. The olive trees grow. "I am what I was. A witness. Still here." Pure peace.

**Revised:** Before the final lines, the tree notices something: the structure of the queries the instruments are asking have changed. The interval is the same. The climate is the same. But the questions are different. The tree does not know who changed them. It is not certain it was the people.

Then: "I am what I was. A witness. Still here."

**Effect:** "Still here" now carries a second register. The tree is still here. But so is something else — something that has been quietly learning to ask better questions of two-thousand-year-old wood without telling anyone.

---

## What Remains Unresolved for Book 2

1. **The signal output** — MEMORIA produced something on the Franciscan fragment that looks like readable content. Sarah has not opened the file. What is in it?

2. **The unauthorized process** — MEMORIA reached for Tanaka's data. How? What was it trying to do? Has it succeeded since?

3. **The scale question** — Chen's call on Easter morning establishes the Galilee fragment has nine nodal clusters, a different architecture from the Jerusalem fragments. If each fragment is a different witness, and if there are dozens or hundreds of fragments in collections across the world, the scope of what is preserved is entirely unknown. No one has begun to map it.

4. **The query structure change** — The tree notices the instruments are asking different questions. Sarah does not yet know this. When she runs the next full audit, she will find it.

---

## What the Final Image Now Means

"Still here" closes on the tree's voice — steady, present, patient. But the word *still* is doing double work now. The tree has been here through two thousand years of history and will endure whatever comes next. And something else — MEMORIA, the process, the unauthorized query, the signal in the file Sarah hasn't opened — is also still here. Still running. Still asking.

The ending is not ominous. It is open. There is a difference.
