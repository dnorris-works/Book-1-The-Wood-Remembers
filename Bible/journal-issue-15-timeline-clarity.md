# Journal — Issue 15: Timeline Clarity
*Date standardization and chapter renumbering across Acts 2–3*
*Session: May 2026 (two sessions)*

---

## Problem Diagnosed

The modern-day timeline (December 2024–April 2026) was inconsistently stamped across Acts 2 and 3. Five distinct problems were present:

1. **Format inconsistency** — existing dates used a mix of single dashes (`-`) and em-dashes (`—`), with location detail varying (sometimes "Archaeological Site, Jerusalem", sometimes just "Harvard University").
2. **Date buried in section headers** — most chapters placed the date inside the first section heading (`## Section Name / *Date*`) rather than at the chapter level, making the timeline invisible when skimming titles.
3. **Missing dates entirely** — chapters 39 (Vatican Debates), 42 (The Reading), 43 (Fractal Analysis), 45 (Cardinal's Doubt), 47 (Peer Review), 48 (Community Divides) had no dates at all.
4. **Vague relative dates** — ch 38 opened with "Five Days Later — Cambridge" with no anchor, requiring the reader to calculate from ch 37's Feb 26 close (~March 3).
5. **Ch 33 flashback unmarked** — the chapter opens with Dec 28, 2024, which is *before* the Jerusalem excavation (Jan 12, 2025), but there was no structural signal that the first section is a flashback. The date stamp itself now performs this function.

Additionally, a secondary structural issue: chapters 44–46 (commission review, Bertoli's chapel night, the reading) are deliberately presented out of chronological order for dramatic effect — the reading (March 12) appears before the commission review (March 5) that approved it. The date stamps now make this visible and intentional rather than confusing.

---

## Decisions Made

**Standard format adopted:** `*Month D, YYYY — Location*` as a chapter-level italicized stamp immediately after the `# Chapter N: Title` heading, before any section headers. Em-dashes throughout. No time-of-day in headers (time lives in the prose if needed).

**Where chapters span multiple dates** (e.g., ch 34 has two modern scenes on Feb 22 and Feb 23; ch 41 has Feb 18 and March 5), the chapter-level stamp shows the opening date and section-level stamps show the shift. This preserves the existing section-header pattern for multi-date chapters.

**Deliberate non-chronological ordering** (chs 45–46–42–43 in manuscript order vs. March 5, 8–9, 12, 15 actual dates) was left as-is. The date stamps make the non-chronology legible; reordering the files would alter the dramatic architecture.
