**The Wood Remembers** *Book One of The Faithful Witness Series*

I existed before I was a tree.

In 87 BCE, a seed lodges in a limestone crack on a hillside outside Jerusalem and begins to grow — aware, patient, and watching. Two millennia later, in a Vatican laboratory, a researcher named Sarah and an AI-assisted physicist stumble onto something impossible: a fragment of wood, long venerated as a relic of the True Cross, that holds structured, memory-like information encoded at the quantum level — architecture that looks less like decay and more like witness.

Moving between the olive tree's first-person account of the centuries it has silently endured — carried through catacombs, hidden through empires, passed hand to hand by those who could not let it go into nothing — and a present-day reckoning inside the Church and the scientific community, *The Wood Remembers* asks what it would mean if matter itself could remember. If faith and physics were never actually at odds, but two instruments recording the same impossible signal.

A novel about consciousness, custody, and the weight of what we choose to carry.
