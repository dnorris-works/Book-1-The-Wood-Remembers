*The Quantum Cross — Book 1 of The Quantum Witness Series*

---

## Genre Summary

Religious Thriller — specifically the subgenre sometimes shelved as Christian Fiction / Supernatural Thriller.

The book sits closest to James Rollins (scientific discovery + religious artifact) and Dan Brown (Vatican + ancient relic + academic protagonist under pressure) but is considerably more literary than either, and more theologically serious than the thriller genre usually allows. The tree-voice sections in particular push it toward Literary Fiction — but that category won't serve it commercially.

---

## The Dan Brown Problem

The Dan Brown comparison will be unavoidable in marketing copy and is worth leaning into rather than resisting, with the qualifier that this book takes both the science and the theology seriously where Brown treats both as plot machinery. The positioning line is something like: *for readers who loved the premise of The Da Vinci Code but wanted it to mean something.*

---

## Comparable Authors / Also-Bought Cluster

- **James Rollins** (Sigma Force series) — for the scientific-discovery-meets-ancient-relic thriller readership
- **Dan Brown** — unavoidable comp, use with qualifier
- **Shuggie Bain / Paul Lynch** — for the literary voice with religious weight
- **Colm Tóibín** (The Testament of Mary) — for readers who want the Gospels taken seriously as literature

The actual sweet spot is readers who felt let down by the shallowness of *The Da Vinci Code* but loved the premise.

---

## Series Positioning

Book 1 of The Quantum Witness Series. The series arc (MEMORIA's autonomous behavior, Tanaka's fragment network, the decoding problem) positions future books deeper into the Supernatural Thriller category as the science becomes more destabilizing. Book 1 earns the thriller classification; subsequent books will inhabit it more fully.
