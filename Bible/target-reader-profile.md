# Target Reader

Readers who found *The Da Vinci Code* compelling but intellectually insulting. Practicing Christian or spiritually curious but not hostile to faith. Reads nonfiction about quantum physics, consciousness, or religious history for pleasure. High tolerance for theological argument inside narrative. More likely 40–65 than younger. Finishes books.

They will find the book through Christian Suspense & Thriller categories. The literary fiction audience finds it through word of mouth.
