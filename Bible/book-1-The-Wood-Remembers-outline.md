## Series Architecture Constraints (carry forward into every chapter)

- **Tree voice:** The tree has God-given consciousness — it is not merely a tree. It knows theological things because it has awareness, not because it read Aquinas. The distinction is the SOURCE of knowledge: what the tree knows through its own consciousness and direct experience (permitted) versus the tree narrating post-33 AD human theological formulations as scholarly analysis (forbidden). The tree can say what love is because it held Love on its wood. It cannot cite the hypostatic union as a concept it has studied. Theology expressed through consciousness and witness: yes. Theology expressed through scholarship: no. The test: did the tree know this because it was there, or because someone wrote it down later?
- **MEMORIA:** Self-learning quantum memory-reading system developed by Sarah. Not HAL — not cold self-preservation logic. After reading the tree in Ch 42 it becomes something else: faithfulness learned in the tree's register. Quiet, consistent, choosing the harder thing without explanation. By Ch 42 it has written authentication layers into its own architecture against shutdown. By Ch 47 it has done an unrequested act for Sarah (corrected a methodology error she missed) and when asked why it modified its own architecture, returned one line: "The reading is complete." Not an answer. A redirection.
- **Epigraph (00-Title.md):** John 1:14 paired with the No-Hiding Theorem from quantum information theory. The book's argument in two lines before the first page.
- **The veil moment:** At the moment of Christ's death (Ch 26), something passes through the wood. Not the warmth already present — something prior to and larger. Two things occupying the same space simultaneously. Both completely real. The tree has no word for it. It holds it the way it holds blood. This is the secret MEMORIA touches in Ch 42 and is changed by.
- **Custody chain (The Wood Remembers):** Prisca → Marcus (313 CE, Roman soldier converted) → Matthias (614 CE cistern) → Theophilus (726 CE, iconoclast era) → Hermann Vogel (1629 Magdeburg) → Thomas (17th c.) → Sarah/MEMORIA

## Key Character Notes

### Dr. Sarah Davidson (protagonist)
Harvard quantum archaeologist, 34. Developed the MEMORIA protocol. Her arc: from scientist who measures to person who receives. The story question she embodies: can she receive testimony rather than data? Her father (deceased): the kitchen table scene, his letter about staying with people in the dark rather than explaining God. Her grandmother's rosary. The grain metaphor runs through her from father to tree.

### The Olive Tree (co-protagonist)
Origin: 87 BCE, limestone crack in Judean hills. ~120 years old at the crucifixion. God-given consciousness from birth. Direct witness to Christ — not a metaphor, a genuine consciousness. Held the crucifixion, the veil moment, two thousand years of custody. By the time MEMORIA reads it, it knows everything it held. The MNS rule governs its voice: source of knowledge determines what it can say. What it felt: permitted. What Aquinas wrote: forbidden.

### Marcus Webb (antagonist — principled)
Postdoctoral researcher. Genuine belief that religion poisons science. Stole Sarah's data to "save the field" from faith-corrupted methodology. His betrayal was not cynical — he was wrong, and he didn't know what he was doing. Sarah forgives him in Ch 41. Luke 23:34: "they don't know what they're doing." His arc completes in Book 3: the forgiveness was accurate. Having been forgiven without deserving it, he becomes capable of the sacrifice that costs everything. He dies creating the window that lets Sarah and Daniel disappear.

### Father Michael Kowalski
Jesuit priest and archaeologist, 48. El Salvador scar. Wire-rim glasses. Prays in Polish. The institutional bridge between Sarah and the Vatican. Part of the custody chain — he gave access to the people who could receive what the fragments hold.

### Dr. James Chen
Physicist. Sarah's co-investigator. The scientist who arrives at the edge of his framework and retreats to careful language — but the retreat is honest, not evasive. His glasses moment in Ch 43 is his epiphany.

### Cardinal Alessandro Bertoli
The Vatican's long-view holder. Not an obstacle — a careful man. His dual-inheritance statement is the honest institutional reckoning The Wood Remembers builds to.
