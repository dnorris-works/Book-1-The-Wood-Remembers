# Cover Concept Brief — The Wood Remembers
*Approved by David, May 2026*

## Decision locked
Retiring the glowing-cross-and-sunburst design (`cover.jpg`) in favor of a minimalist single-motif cover: one gold olive branch on flat black, whose crossing twigs read as a cross on a second look rather than a literal crucifix. Rationale: 2026 cover trend research favors one dominant color + one strong motif + confident type over busy photographic/stock-glow designs for literary/Christian-thriller crossover fiction; see chat notes for full research summary.

## Files
- `cover-concept-v1-olive-branch.svg` — approved concept, digital/eBook proportions (1600×2560 px, 1:1.6 ratio — matches KDP eBook cover spec). Vector, editable in Affinity.
- `cover.jpg` — old title, retained for reference only until replaced.

## Spec for eBook cover (ready to redraw in Affinity at full resolution)
- **Canvas:** 1600×2560 px (or any multiple of the 1:1.6 ratio) — flat single-panel, no spine.
- **Background:** flat `#0A0908` (near-black), no gradient, no vignette.
- **Motif:** single olive branch, vertical stem with two crossing side-branches near the upper third (suggesting a cross without being one), simple leaf shapes, small olive cluster. Gold `#C9A24A`, leaves slightly darker `#B08A3E`, olives `#8A6A2E`.
- **Kicker (top):** "THE FAITHFUL WITNESS SERIES · BOOK ONE" — small caps, tracked out, `#A79C87`.
- **Title:** "THE WOOD REMEMBERS" stacked two lines, bold serif (Georgia/Times-style), cream `#EFE6D3`.
- **Subtitle (kept per David's direction):** short evocative line, italic serif, `#A79C87` — e.g. "A Harvard archaeologist reads what a two-thousand-year-old witness remembers." (Full keyword-rich KDP subtitle stays in the metadata field only, not on the cover art.)
- **Author name:** "D. ALLEN NORRIS" bold, gold `#D4AF37`, bottom third — treated as a brand element (larger/bolder than typical debut-author treatment), per current thriller-cover convention.
- **Fonts:** serif throughout (Georgia or a licensed equivalent like Adobe Caslon / EB Garamond in the final Affinity build) — no sans-serif anywhere on this cover.
