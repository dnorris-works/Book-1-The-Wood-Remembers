## Format & Architecture

### Daily Structure (Per Week)

| Day | Voice | Type | Notes |
|-----|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | The Tree | Opening witness | Theology + quantum science |
| 2 | You (Reader) | On receiving | Psychology + scripture |
| 3 | Sarah | The threshold | Psychology + quantum science |
| 4 | You (Reader) | On resistance or doubt | Psychology + scripture |
| 5 | The Tree | Second image | Theology + quantum science |
| 6 | The Larger Frame | Synthesis — scripture, doctrine, science, psychology | All four layers |
| 7 | Silence | One question, no answer | No notes — silence stays clean |

### Note Layers (Days 1–6)
- **Scripture** — chapter and verse, with translation noted
- **Christian doctrine** — primary sources cited (Aquinas, Augustine, the mystics, Reformation confessions)
- **Verified science** — quantum physics, cosmology, epigenetics, neuroscience
- **Psychology** — Jung, Frankl, Curt Thompson, Daniel Siegel, James Pennebaker

### Voice Register
- **Warm and accessible** — wide audience, no assumed theological background
- **Theologically serious** — doctrine present but never heavy-handed
- **The tree's voice** — slightly wider than strict MNS; may name what it felt theologically
- **Sarah's voice** — the scientist at the threshold; honest about doubt
- **Reader voice** — interior, not instructional; invites inward rather than upward
